# Demo: The Road Home — beat chart

Mission `road-home`. Map generated by `tools/road-home-level.mjs`, script in
`src/content/missions/road-home.json`, tests in `tests/road-home.test.ts`. Made under
[../WORKFLOW.md](../WORKFLOW.md): the owner gave the vision, the agent chose everything else.

## The owner's brief (2026-11-10)

> Start with a solemn introduction to the mission with emotional beats from your teammates. There
> is a world-building element, as much as is reasonable with our current assets. Enemies are at
> first sparse and unaware of you, eventually culminating in a garrison fight before mission end.
> The climax should be steadily building toward the mission end.

## The agent's premise

Six years ago the Directorate ran its Wall through the village of Lipovka, turned the villagers out
in one night (forty minutes to pack, a form to sign) and billeted its border clerks in their
houses. Pasha, the driver, grew up there. Tonight tank 412 drives home through it, by lanes he
knows, to the Lipovka gate, which only opens from this side.

Why this premise: the route hints the driver always gives become the emotional beats (he knows the
ditch because he played in it); an occupied sleeping village explains sparse, unaware guards; the
gate explains a fight that cannot be sneaked past; and it needs nothing the renderer lacks. The lit
windows, Directorate posters and the church bell in the score are already there, and the script
points at them.

## The arrangement

One line that only goes up. Each stretch adds one enemy or one kind of pressure, the crew's talk
thins as the danger grows, and nothing comes after the peak except driving through the gate.

| # | Stretch | Purpose | Enemies | Pressure | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Orchard lane | Tone. Nothing to do but drive and listen | 0 | none | Six years; the eviction; the orchards kept growing. Optional: the Gromov orchard |
| S2 | Lipovka village | First enemy, seen from far off, easy to avoid | 1 static | leave the street for a back lane | "There's a light on in our kitchen." |
| S3 | Church and school | Two enemies that never look at the same place | 1 static, 1 patrol | choose a side: churchyard (safe) or behind the school (timed) | The bell is ours; the graves are the only ones allowed to stay |
| S4 | The raked strip | First stretch with no fully safe line | 2 patrolling together | two exposed gaps in the hedgerow; suspicion rises and drains | The Commander watched this happen from the far bank |
| S5 | Supply yard | "From here they are awake." One quiet route left | 3 | the ditch or a fight | Pasha played in that ditch |
| S6 | The gate | The fight. No way round | 3, then 2, then 1 heavy | three waves, each called by the last | No jokes tonight. "Going home." |

- **The fight (S6).** Gate patrol and two gate posts. When two of the three are down, two reserves
  leave the barracks. When both reserves are down, Hauptmann Reuter (120 hp, skill 0.75) comes
  through the gate itself. Each wave sets a checkpoint, so a death never replays a finished wave.
  No repair inside the fight: damage carries, which is the pressure.
- **The ending.** The gate opens only when all six are destroyed; then it is ten tiles to the end.
  The optional objective is to reach the gate unseen; it changes the last line, not the fight.
- **Checkpoints with field repair** after S2, S3, S4 and S5; a checkpoint without repair after S1.

## What was checked without a human

- Driven flat out, S1 takes 16 s; on silent running, 39 s. No guard can see any of it.
- Peak guard suspicion on each stretch's quiet line: S1 0, S2 0, S3 0 to 0.3, S4 about 0.4,
  S5 about 0.45. The road itself gets you spotted in S2, S4 and S5.
- Every checkpoint is out of sight of every guard; an alarm stays inside its stretch.
- The three waves fire in order; a retry mid-fight keeps the kills.

## Not judged: questions for the playtest

1. **Does the opening breathe?** The first threat is about 40 s in at speed. Is that long enough
   to feel solemn, or do the lines pile up while you are already driving?
2. **Do the lines land?** 34 radio lines. Which ones would you cut, and is any crew member off-voice?
3. **Is the build steady?** Name the first stretch where you felt in danger. If it is S2 or S6,
   the curve is wrong in the middle.
4. **Is S5 a step or a stall?** It has the least story. It may want its own beat or to be shorter.
5. **Is the fight the right size?** Six tanks in three waves, no repair. Too long, too short, and
   does the Hauptmann feel like the peak or like one more tank?
6. **Does the ending arrive too fast** after the last kill, or is the short drive right?

## Known limits of the current assets

Village houses and the church are the same grey blocks as the city; there are no trees (the
orchards are rows of brush), no wrecks or ruins as scenery, and only the crew can speak on the
radio with a portrait. The world-building is carried by the lines, the lit windows, the posters
and graffiti, the Wall and the bell.
